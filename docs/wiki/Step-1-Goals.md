# Step 1: Day 80 goals

**Question:** What is the story, what will you learn, and which data does the project use?

![Step 1: the two data tables](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/080-Dr-Semmelweis-Handwashing-Discovery/raw/branch/main/docs/images/step-1-goals.png)

## What the step does

Lecture 591 tells the story of Dr Ignaz Semmelweis (born 1818), who worked at the Vienna General Hospital. Many women in the maternity wards died of childbed fever (puerperal fever). Semmelweis suspected the hospital's procedures and collected data from 1841 to 1849. The lecture gives you two files: `annual_deaths_by_clinic.csv` (births and deaths per year and clinic) and `monthly_deaths.csv` (births and deaths per month). The picture shows the first rows of both.

## What you gained

- How to make a compelling argument using data.
- How to superimpose histograms to show differences in distributions.
- How to use a Kernel Density Estimate (KDE) to show a graphic estimate of a distribution.
- How to use SciPy and test for statistical significance by looking at p-values.
- How to highlight different parts of a time series chart in Matplotlib, and add a legend.
- How to use NumPy's `where()` to process elements depending on a condition.
